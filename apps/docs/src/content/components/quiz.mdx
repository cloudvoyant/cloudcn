Quiz (diffbook's `Quiz`) composes a `title` and a list of questions into a single quiz: answer each question, then hit **Submit** to grade them all at once and see the per-question results and total score simultaneously. Each question is a discriminated union (`type: 'single' | 'multiple' | 'numeric'`).
